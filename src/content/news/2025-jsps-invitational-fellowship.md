Dr. Wenwu Xu received the FY2024 Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) Invitational Fellowship for Research in Japan. The fellowship supported a 32-day research collaboration in Spring 2025 with host Professor Dr. Motomichi Koyama at Tohoku University, advancing international research exchange in materials science and modeling.
