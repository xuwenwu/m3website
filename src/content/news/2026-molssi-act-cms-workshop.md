Dr. Wenwu Xu attended the MolSSI ACT-CMS workshop at the Molecular Sciences Software Institute near Virginia Tech in Blacksburg, Virginia. As a 2025-2027 MolSSI ACT-CMS Faculty Fellow, Dr. Xu is developing "Transforming Materials Modeling Education through LLM-Assisted Coding and HPC Integration for SDSU's Doctoral Engineering Program." The workshop supports faculty fellows in integrating programming, computation, and reusable curriculum modules into molecular and materials science education.
