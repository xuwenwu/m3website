Diego Cea and Isaiah Clemons, Mechanical Engineering juniors, joined the Multiscale Modeling of Materials Lab through the SDSU MESA Summer Undergraduate Research Academy 2026. During this eight-week, faculty-led research experience, they are working on Thermal-Elastic Processing experiments and characterization, including sample preparation and coupled thermal-mechanical testing, with support from SDSU MESA and MESA California.
